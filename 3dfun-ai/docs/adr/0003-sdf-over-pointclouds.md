# ADR 0003: Signed Distance Fields Over Point Clouds & Radiance Fields for Watertight Topology

## Status
Accepted

## Context
Single-image 3D reconstruction systems traditionally output one of three geometric representations:
1. **Dense Point Clouds:** Generated by unprojecting depth pixels into 3D space.
2. **Neural Radiance Fields (NeRF) / 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS):** Representing view-dependent color densities.
3. **Implicit Volumetric Fields (SDF / Occupancy Grids):** Representing surfaces as level sets of continuous functions.

Physical engineering, additive manufacturing (3D printing), and physical simulation require closed, orientable, watertight 2-manifold surfaces with positive interior volume. Point clouds lack connectivity, and radiance fields generate semi-transparent "floaters" without explicit surface boundaries.

## Decision
We adopted a **discrete Signed Distance Field (SDF)** derived via exact Euclidean Distance Transform (EDT) over a constrained occupancy slab, followed by standard Marching Cubes surface extraction.

## Consequences
### Positive:
- **Provable Watertightness (Lemma 1):** Boundary Dirichlet conditions ensure that the zero-crossing isosurface is strictly interior to the volume, guaranteeing zero boundary edges ($\partial\mathcal{M} = \emptyset$).
- **Direct 3D Printing Compatibility:** Generated meshes can be sliced directly in Cura or PrusaSlicer without topological repair.
- **Efficient Extraction:** Marching cubes on calibrated grids ($72^3$ voxels) extracts $8\,000$-triangle surfaces in under $220$ ms.

### Negative:
- Memory footprint scales cubically with grid resolution ($O(N^3)$), requiring adaptive grid calibration to maintain performance.
